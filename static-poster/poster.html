RWANDA CIVIL REGISTRATION & VITAL STATISTICS · 2019–2025

WHO GETS COUNTED?

Rwanda records nearly every birth through its health system. But when citizens die, half the registry goes blank. Here is the data story behind Rwanda’s 50.5% death recording gap.

13.9 pp Gap: Female death completeness (43.7%) severely lags male completeness (57.6%). Of the 38,521 unregistered deaths in Rwanda, 22,310 (57.9%) are female, driven by inheritance and land transfer rules that prioritize male death certificates.

Policy Actions to Reach ≥80% Completeness by 2030

1. Mobile CHW Reporting

Equip Community Health Workers to file digital death notices at the village level, removing travel barriers for rural families.

2. Audit Female Death Barriers

Decouple death registration from male inheritance disputes and waive all late fees for registering deceased mothers and daughters.

3. Age-Specific Denominators

NISR should publish census-projected expected deaths by age group to reveal exact age-specific under-registration rates.
